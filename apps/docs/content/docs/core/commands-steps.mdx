---
title: "Commands and Steps"
description: "Define commands, groups, conditional steps and async options."
---

A `Command` has `id`, `label`, optional `when`, `steps`, and either `run(answers)` or nested `commands`. A command with children opens its children after its own steps; it does not execute its `run`. Answers use step IDs as keys; a child step may shadow a parent ID. Use stable, unique IDs within each menu. Search matches labels, not IDs.

## Conditions

`Command.when` controls visibility; `Step.when` controls whether a step is visited. Both receive the current answers; registry commands also receive their owning context.

## Async options

`Step.options` is an array or a loader. A legacy loader returns a promise; contextual registry loaders can return an array or a promise and receive context. While it loads, the snapshot reports `loading`; a failed load exposes `error` and `loadFailed` and can be retried with `retry()`. The engine ignores results made obsolete by navigation, close or destruction, but does not abort the underlying request. Validate network responses in your loader.

**Try it:** run `pnpm dev`, then choose **Steps & conditions** in the playground's *Try it* grid. **Progress & async** loads every step asynchronously, and **Nested commands** shows a child command hidden by `when`.
