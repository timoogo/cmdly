---
title: "Final Steps and Lifecycle"
description: "Review answers before or after execution."
---

A `finalStep` can specify `timing: 'before' | 'after'`, `when`, `fields`, `closeOnComplete`, and `render`. Final settings are inherited by descendants unless overridden or disabled with `false`. A before step can call `execute()`; an after step runs first and then shows its result.

The engine owns no UI. Destroy it when its owner ends, and do not reuse an engine after close. Plugins are cleaned up on destroy.
